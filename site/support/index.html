Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my progress? Settings -> Data & Account -> Reset progress. It erases your progress on this device and keeps your cloud backup; use Restore from Backup in the same screen to bring the backup back.

How do I change my avatar? Settings -> Identity, then tap your avatar to open the picker and choose Camera, Gallery or a Sigil. Photos you pick are processed and stored on your device only.

How do I restore purchases? On the main Settings screen, in the habitGate: Level up system Premium card, tap Restore Purchases (the same button is on the upgrade screen). Purchases are re-checked with Google Play through RevenueCat; if nothing is found, the app says so.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.

How do I report a bug? Email monarchlabsapps@gmail.com with your app version and device model — or use Report a Bug in Settings -> About, which attaches your message, app version, platform, OS version and a device identifier.
