Subscription and Purchases Disclosure

Last updated: 2026-11-01 · Version 0.1-draft

This page is the public version of the disclosure shown in the purchase screen inside habitGate: Level up system. The price, billing period and renewal terms for your region are also shown by Google Play before you confirm any purchase.

The core game is free

habitGate: Level up system's full game loop — quests, the Daily Directive, emergency protocols, the 30 catalog dungeons, the dungeon creator, XP, levels, ranks, stats, streaks, guest mode and the coin Store — is free. Premium adds convenience and cosmetics; it never unlocks power and never gates the core loop.

What Premium includes

One subscription ("habitGate: Level up system Premium") with three benefits:

  • Cloud Backup and Sync — multi-device sync and restore on a new phone. Without Premium, progress is stored on your device only.
  • Premium cosmetics — a fixed set of exclusive skins, titles and avatar cosmetics. Cosmetic only; there are no XP or stat multipliers.
  • Advanced insights — deeper statistics: XP trend analytics, the activity heatmap and data export.

Auto-renewal

Monthly and yearly subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal is charged by Google through your Google Play payment method. The Lifetime tier is a one-time purchase and never renews.

How to cancel

Cancel any time in Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → habitGate: Level up system. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the Google Play subscription — cancel in Google Play first.

Refunds

Google Play offers a standard refund window for recent purchases: 48 hours for app purchases, and subscription terms as set by Google Play's policy. Request refunds through Google Play → order history or Google Play support. A refund granted by Google revokes Premium access, including any benefits already used. For anything outside Google's automated flow, contact monarchlabsapps@gmail.com.

Payments processing

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card details. We receive only the entitlement status — which plan is active and until when — validated through RevenueCat, so that Premium features can be enabled on your account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
